**Description:**
Curzi is the currency converter the way it used to feel. Keep the currencies you actually use in one list, tap any amount, type, and every other currency updates at once. Pin one to see its rate under every row. Drag to reorder. Rates are mid-market, refreshed daily, and cached so the app works offline.

A built-in calculator keypad means you can type 5×100÷7 straight into an amount.

Growth works out what regular saving comes to: starting balance, return, additions weekly to yearly, inflation, and the value in today's money, with a chart and a year-by-year table. Switch to Withdraw to see how long a balance lasts when you draw it down. Save scenarios and compare them side by side.

Mortgage shows the payment for any price, down payment, rate and amortization, with semi-annual (Canada) or monthly (US) compounding, accelerated bi-weekly and weekly payments, what you owe at the end of your term, and what overpayments save you in time and interest.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Curzi is free; a one-time Support purchase says thanks and keeps it that way.
